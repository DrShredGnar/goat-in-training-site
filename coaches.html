GOAT in Training

For coaches

You get your players two or three times a week. GOAT in Training gives them a short session for the other days, at home with a parent, so the reps keep going between practices.

  • It works alongside practice. Three weekday sessions build speed, power and footwork. One is skill work for your sport, Friday is rest, and on Sunday a game or a team practice counts as the session.
  • Nothing for you to run. A parent sets it up on their own phone. There are no team accounts to manage, no chat and no public profiles, and nothing a child enters is shown to anyone else.
  • Free to try. The first 7 sessions are free for every athlete: one full week. After that, one family membership covers every child in the house, whatever each one plays.

What your players would be doing

Every drill has plain steps, a starting amount and one thing for the parent to say, for ages 6 to 14. You can read all 168 of them here before you recommend anything: football, basketball, baseball and softball, hockey and soccer, and the speed, power and footwork days every sport shares.

The plan is general training guidance and not medical advice. It was not written by your league or reviewed by a governing body, so read the drills and decide for yourself.
