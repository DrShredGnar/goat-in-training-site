GOAT in Training

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

GOAT in Training is a training app for young athletes that a parent or guardian sets up and manages. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers the mobile app and this website.

The short version

  • A parent's email address and the training information you enter are all we keep.
  • No ads. No selling or renting information. No tracking or analytics tools. No location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • Nothing a child enters is ever shown to other people. There are no public profiles, messages or leaderboards.
  • You can use the app without an account. In that case your family's information stays on your device. The only things we receive are feedback you choose to send and a short technical report if the app hits an error.
  • You can delete an athlete, or your whole account, from inside the app at any time.
  • If you join the waitlist on this website, we keep that email address only to tell you once that the app is ready.

If you join the waitlist on this website

Before the app is in the stores, the home page has a form where a parent can ask to be told when it is ready. If you use it, we keep the email address you typed and the date. It is not linked to an account or to any athlete. We use it to send you one email saying the app is available, and for nothing else. The form is for parents and guardians, and it does not ask for anything about a child.

Technical information

When the app or the waitlist form talks to our servers, our hosting provider processes the device's internet (IP) address and basic request details, as every online service does. We use this only to deliver the app and keep it secure. We do not use advertising identifiers, cookies for tracking, or any identifier to follow you across other apps or websites.

Reminders

A parent can turn on a daily reminder in the parent area. The reminder is made by the app on that phone. We do not send it from our servers, and we do not collect a notification address for your device. Once notifications are allowed, a drill timer can also tell you that a round is over while the phone is locked. That is made on the phone in the same way.

Feedback and error reports

  • Feedback you send. The parent area, which is behind the parent PIN, has a feedback box. If you write something and press Send, we receive what you wrote, the app version and the kind of device. If you are signed in, it is linked to your account so we can reply. If you are not, you can add an email address for a reply or leave it blank.
  • Error reports. If the app hits an error, it sends us a short technical report: what went wrong, the app version and the kind of device. The report is not linked to an account and does not include names, email addresses or training information. It is the same whether or not you are signed in.

What we do not collect

We do not collect last names, home addresses, phone numbers, school names, photos, video, voice recordings, precise location, contacts, or anything from social media. The only free-text box is the feedback box in the parent area, behind the parent PIN. The screens a child uses have no chat, comment or other free-text fields.

How we use information

  • To run the app: show sessions, track progress, and keep your family's phones in sync.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To know whether your family has a membership.
  • To answer you when you contact us.
  • To email you once when the app is available, if you joined the waitlist.

We do not use information for advertising, we do not build profiles for marketing, and we do not sell or rent information to anyone.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children, including children under 13, to use together with a parent or guardian. Accounts belong to the parent. Children do not create accounts and are never asked for contact information.

  • What we collect from children. When a child uses the app, we collect only the training activity and Combine Day results described above, attached to the first name or nickname the parent chose.
  • No public sharing. A child cannot make any information publicly available through the app. The parent area, behind the parent PIN and a question for grown-ups, can make a picture of an athlete's progress for a parent to send with their phone's own share sheet. The parent chooses who gets it and whether the name is on it. The app itself sends it nowhere.
  • Face or fingerprint unlock. A parent can choose to open the parent area with their phone's face or fingerprint check. The phone does that check and tells the app only yes or no. The app never sees or stores a face or a fingerprint.
  • No more than needed. We do not ask a child for more information than is needed to use the app.
  • Consent. A child's information is saved to our servers only after a parent creates an account with their own email address. Until then it stays on the device.

A parent's rights

As the parent or guardian you can, at any time:

  • Review your child's information. All of it is visible in the app on the Today, Climb, Combine and Levels screens.
  • Delete it. In the Parent area, "Remove this athlete" deletes that athlete's sessions and results, and "Delete account" deletes your account and everything in it.
  • Refuse further collection. Sign out and delete the account, or use the app without an account, and no further information about your child is sent to us.

You can also ask us to do any of these by emailing support@goatintraining.com or calling 978-503-9324. We will confirm the request through the email address on the account before acting on it.

How long we keep information

  • We keep account information for as long as the account exists.
  • Removing an athlete deletes that athlete's training days and Combine Day results from our servers straight away.
  • Deleting your account deletes the account and everything in it from our servers straight away. Backup copies are overwritten within 30 days.
  • If an account is not used for 24 months, we delete it and everything in it.
  • We delete the waitlist within 30 days of sending the email that says the app is available. To be taken off sooner, email support@goatintraining.com.

How we protect information

Information is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers. Our database only lets a signed-in parent read or change their own family's records. The parent PIN is stored hashed. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your account.

Your choices and rights

You can correct information in the app, delete it as described above, or ask us for a copy. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under your state's law, such as the right to know, correct, delete, or appeal a decision about your request. Email support@goatintraining.com to use any of them. We will not treat you differently for doing so.
