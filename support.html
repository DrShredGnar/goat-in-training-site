Support

Help with GOAT in Training.

Email support@goatintraining.com and we will get back to you. Please write from the email address on your account so we can find it.

Signing in

I did not get the sign-in code

Check your spam folder, make sure the address was typed correctly, and wait a minute before asking for a new one. Codes expire, so use the newest email.

Can I sign in on this website?

No. GOAT in Training runs on your phone, and setting up and signing in both happen inside the app.

How do I get my athletes onto a second phone?

Open the app on the second phone and sign in with the same parent email, either on the first screen or in the Parent tab. Your athletes and their progress appear after it syncs.

I forgot the parent PIN

There is no PIN reset in the app yet. Email us from your account's email address and we will help.

Membership

How do I cancel?

Memberships are managed by the store you bought from.

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play, then your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions.

Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

I paid but the app is still locked

In the Parent tab, tap "Restore purchases". If your athlete uses a different phone, sign in on both phones with the same parent email.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Request one through your purchase history in the store.

Your data

How do I delete an athlete or my account?

In the Parent tab, "Remove this athlete" deletes one athlete's sessions and results. "Delete account" deletes your account and everything in it, on every phone. You can also ask us to do it by email. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Training

Is this safe for my child?

The plan uses body weight, with an optional band and light medicine ball from age 12, and follows general pediatric guidance on rest and volume, but it is not medical advice. Supervise every session, stop if anything hurts, and check with your child's doctor before starting. The Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
