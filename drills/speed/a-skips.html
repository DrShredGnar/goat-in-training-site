A-skips

A speed drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Mark out 15 yards. Jog down and back twice to get warm.
  2. A-skip: skip forward, lifting one knee to hip height with your toes pulled up. Then drive that foot straight down so it lands under your hips.
  3. Stay tall. Elbows bent, hands swinging from cheek to pocket, close to your sides.
  4. Walk back after each one. Finish with two build-up runs, the second a little faster.

How much to do

4 × 15 yd to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Jog first. Then knee up, toe up, and foot down under your hips. Walk it before you skip it.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as A-skips and becomes A-skips and wall drives; A and B skips; A, B and C skips.

When to move up: every rep at full speed, with no slowing before the line, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Monday is speed day.

Speed day. Train like a sprinter: practise how you run, then short, all-out sprints with full rest. Speed gets built when you are fresh.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
