Power skips

A speed drill, best from age 9. For a younger athlete it is the last drill of the session, and optional at the weekend.

How to do it

  1. Skip forward, but push off the ground as hard as you can.
  2. Drive one knee up to hip height and punch the opposite arm up.
  3. Land softly on the ball of your foot and go straight into the next skip.

How much to do

4 × 20 yd to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Skip for height. Big knee, big arm.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Power skips and becomes Bounding.

When to move up: every rep at full speed, with no slowing before the line, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Monday is speed day.

Speed day. Train like a sprinter: practise how you run, then short, all-out sprints with full rest. Speed gets built when you are fresh.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
