Reaction starts

A speed drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Stand at the start line in a ready stance.
  2. Watch or listen for Coach's signal.
  3. The moment it comes, sprint 10 yards through the line.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

Why it helps

Reaction starts practice responding to a signal instead of a countdown. That matters in sports where the start is a whistle, a snap or a ball being hit, not a planned "go."

The usual mistake

Kids often guess and leave before the clap. Vary the wait each rep, and if they go early, just reset and try again with no fuss.

Make it easier

Keep the wait short and the same each time at first, then start mixing it up.

How much to do

10 × 10 yd to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Go on a clap.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every rep at full speed, with no slowing before the line, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Saturday is full practice, the longest session of the week.

Saturday practice. Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then the sport itself.
