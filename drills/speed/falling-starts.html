Falling starts

A speed drill for ages 12 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Mark a start line and a line 10 yards away.
  2. Stand tall at the start, feet together. Lean forward from your ankles, body straight, until you have to step.
  3. Drive out and sprint. Push the ground back behind you and stay leaning for the first five or six steps.
  4. Come up slowly, never all at once. Walk back and rest until your breathing is easy.

How much to do

6 × 10 yd to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Lean until you fall into the first step, then sprint. Stay low for five steps.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Falling starts and becomes Two-point starts, Three-point starts and Push-up starts.

When to move up: every rep at full speed, with no slowing before the line, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Monday is speed day.

From age 12. At 12 the speed day becomes a 45-minute session that starts with a control warm-up and adds these.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
