Stretch

A rest-day stretching routine for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Hips: kneel on one knee and push your hips forward.
  2. Hamstrings: sit with one leg straight and reach for your toes.
  3. Calves: hands on a wall, one leg back, heel down.
  4. Shoulders: pull one arm across your chest. Hold each stretch 20 to 30 seconds per side.

How much to do

8 min every time. This one does not change with the level.

What to say: Hips, hamstrings, calves, shoulders. Hold each one and breathe slowly.

Where it fits in the week

Friday is the rest day: no running or jumping.

On the rest day. No running or jumping today. Rest is when your body gets stronger, and smart players win.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
