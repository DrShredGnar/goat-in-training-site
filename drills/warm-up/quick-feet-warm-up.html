Quick-feet warm-up

A warm-up for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Jump rope for one minute at an easy pace. With no rope, hop on the spot as if you had one.
  2. Ladder or chalk boxes, down and back twice: one foot in each box, then two feet in each box.
  3. Line hops: 20 quick hops forward and back over a line, then 20 side to side.
  4. Carioca 10 yards down and back, leading with each side.
  5. How to carioca, also called grapevine or karaoke: move sideways, crossing your back foot in front, then behind, then in front again. Your hips twist and your shoulders stay facing forward. Lead with the other side on the way back.
  6. Finish with two easy 10-yard runs, the second a little faster.

Why it helps

This warm-up gets the feet moving with rope, ladder patterns and line hops before harder work. It is a good fit before a footwork or speed day.

The usual mistake

Kids tend to go so hard on the hops that they are tired before the session starts. Keep it light and quick; the goal is ready, not worn out.

How much to do

4 min every time. This one does not change with the level.

What to say: Easy rope, two ladder patterns, quick line hops, carioca. Light and quick, not tired.

Where it fits in the week

It starts Wednesday's power and body control day, so quick feet are practised on a second day of the week without a longer session.

Before a session. A warm-up is a few minutes of easy movement that gets faster, so the first sprint or jump is not the first hard thing the body does that day.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
