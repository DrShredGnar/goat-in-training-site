Warm-ups and stretching for young athletes

The 4 routines that start a session or fill the rest day, for ages 6 to 14. Each takes four to six minutes and needs no equipment beyond a rope.

Before a session

A warm-up is a few minutes of easy movement that gets faster, so the first sprint or jump is not the first hard thing the body does that day.

  • Dynamic warm-up

    Skips, high knees, butt kicks, side shuffle, carioca. 10 yards each, down and back.

  • Quick-feet warm-up

    Easy rope, two ladder patterns, quick line hops, carioca. Light and quick, not tired.

  • Control warm-up From 12

    Jog, shuffle, carioca, lunge, swing, squat, balance, then jump and land. Knees over toes on every landing.

On the rest day

No running or jumping today. Rest is when your body gets stronger, and smart players win.

  • Stretch

    Hips, hamstrings, calves, shoulders. Hold each one and breathe slowly.
