Control warm-up

A warm-up for ages 12 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Jog 20 yards down and back twice, then side shuffle down and back.
  2. Walking lunges for 10 yards. Your front knee stays over your foot and does not fall inward.
  3. Leg swings: 10 forward and back on each leg, holding a wall or fence.
  4. 10 slow squats, pushing your knees out over your toes.
  5. One-leg balance: 20 seconds on each leg with the knee slightly bent.
  6. 5 jumps straight up. Land quietly on both feet, knees bent and over your toes, and hold for a second.
  7. Finish with two build-up runs of 20 yards, the second one faster.

How much to do

6 min every time. This one does not change with the level.

What to say: Jog, lunge, swing, squat, balance, then jump and land. Knees over toes on every landing.

Where it fits in the week

From age 12 it starts every speed, power and footwork day: balance, knee control and landing practice before anything fast.

Before a session. A warm-up is a few minutes of easy movement that gets faster, so the first sprint or jump is not the first hard thing the body does that day.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
