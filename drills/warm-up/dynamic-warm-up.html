Dynamic warm-up

A warm-up for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Mark out 10 yards.
  2. Go down and back once for each move: skips, high knees, butt kicks, side shuffle, carioca.
  3. How to carioca, also called grapevine or karaoke: move sideways, crossing your back foot in front, then behind, then in front again. Your hips twist and your shoulders stay facing forward. Lead with the other side on the way back.
  4. Finish with two easy 10-yard runs, the second a little faster.

How much to do

4 min every time. This one does not change with the level.

What to say: Skips, high knees, butt kicks, side shuffle, carioca. 10 yards each, down and back.

Where it fits in the week

Thursday's feet and coordination day and Saturday's full practice both start with it.

Before a session. A warm-up is a few minutes of easy movement that gets faster, so the first sprint or jump is not the first hard thing the body does that day.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.

More from Before a session

  • Quick-feet warm-up

    Easy rope, two ladder patterns, quick line hops, carioca. Light and quick, not tired.

  • Control warm-up From 12

    Jog, shuffle, carioca, lunge, swing, squat, balance, then jump and land. Knees over toes on every landing.
