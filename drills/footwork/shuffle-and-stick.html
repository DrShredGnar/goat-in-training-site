Shuffle and stick

A footwork drill for ages 12 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Set two cones 5 yards apart.
  2. Shuffle from one to the other as fast as you can without crossing your feet.
  3. At the cone, stop on your outside leg: knee bent and over your toes, hips back, chest up.
  4. Hold for two seconds without a wobble, then shuffle back and stick the other leg.

How much to do

3 × 4 each side to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Stop on the outside leg and hold for two seconds. No wobble.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Shuffle and stick and becomes Leap and stick and Stick and go.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Thursday is feet and coordination day.

From age 12. At 12 the feet and coordination day becomes a 45-minute session that starts with a control warm-up and adds these.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
