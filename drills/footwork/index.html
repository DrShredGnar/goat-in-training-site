Footwork and agility drills for kids

9 footwork, balance and change-of-direction drills for ages 6 to 14. All they need is a rope, a line on the ground and a few cones or shoes.

Feet and coordination day

Quick feet, balance and changing direction. Stay low and stay light.

  • Jump rope

    Two feet together. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

  • Ladder footwork

    One foot in each box, two feet in each box, sideways shuffle, hopscotch.

  • One-leg balance catch

    Easy tosses right to your chest.

  • 5-10-5 shuttle Best from 9

    Three going right first, three going left first.

  • Mirror drill

    Stay right in front of Coach.

Harder footwork drills

For athletes of 11 and up. They follow the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday, and from age 12 they are part of every feet and coordination day.

  • Plant and cut From 11

    Jog in. Plant, cut and go.

  • Box drill From 11

    Learn the pattern at an easy pace, then speed up.

From age 12

At 12 the feet and coordination day becomes a 45-minute session that starts with a control warm-up and adds these.

  • Shuffle and stick From 12

    Stop on the outside leg and hold for two seconds. No wobble.

  • L run From 12

    Three-quarter speed. Low hips and a hard plant at the corner.
