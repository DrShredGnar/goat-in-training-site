Box drill

A footwork drill for ages 11 to 14. In the app it follows the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday, and from age 12 it is part of every feet and coordination day.

How to do it

  1. Set four cones in a square, 5 yards on each side.
  2. Sprint up the first side, shuffle across the top, backpedal down the far side, and shuffle back to the start.
  3. Face the same way all the way around. Stay low on the shuffles and do not cross your feet.
  4. Rest for as long as the round took.

Why it helps

The box drill links four ways of moving: forward, sideways, backward and sideways again. Sports rarely move in one direction, so this practices switching between them while facing one way.

The usual mistake

Kids often turn their body at the corners instead of facing forward the whole time. Watch for the chest pointing the same way on every side.

Make it easier

Walk the pattern once, then jog it, saying each move out loud at the corners.

How much to do

4 rounds to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Learn the pattern at an easy pace, then speed up.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week
