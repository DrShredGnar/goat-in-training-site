Mirror drill

A footwork drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Set two cones 5 yards apart and face Coach between them.
  2. Coach shuffles side to side and changes direction at any time.
  3. Shuffle to stay right in front. Stay low and do not cross your feet.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

Why it helps

The mirror drill practices reading another person's movement and staying in front of them. It is what defenders do in basketball and soccer, and what kids do in many tag games.

The usual mistake

Kids cross their feet or stand up tall when the direction changes. Watch for a low stance and feet that slide apart and together, never crossing.

Make it easier

Move slowly and in a clear rhythm at first, so they can predict when you will switch.

How much to do

6 × 10 s to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Stay right in front of Coach.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Thursday is feet and coordination day.

Feet and coordination day. Quick feet, balance and changing direction. Stay low and stay light.
