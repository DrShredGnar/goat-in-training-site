Ladder footwork

A footwork drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Lay an agility ladder flat, or draw ten boxes with chalk or tape.
  2. Go through one pattern at a time, slowly at first, on the balls of your feet.
  3. Swing your arms like you are running. Once you know the pattern, look ahead and not at your feet.
  4. Walk back to the start.

Why it helps

Ladder footwork practices placing the feet quickly and exactly where they need to go. That control shows up when a player dodges, dribbles through traffic or steps around a defender.

The usual mistake

Kids speed up before they know the pattern, then miss boxes or stare at their feet. Go slowly until it is clean, then lift the eyes and add speed.

Make it easier

Teach one pattern per session, starting with one foot in each box, and walk it first.

How much to do

4 patterns × 3 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: One foot in each box, two feet in each box, sideways shuffle, hopscotch.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Thursday is feet and coordination day.

Feet and coordination day. Quick feet, balance and changing direction. Stay low and stay light.
