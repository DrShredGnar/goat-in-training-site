Plant and cut

A footwork drill for ages 11 to 14. In the app it follows the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday, and from age 12 it is part of every feet and coordination day.

How to do it

  1. Set one cone 5 yards in front of you.
  2. Run at the cone. Just before it, plant your outside foot and sink your hips.
  3. Push off that foot and cut away at an angle. Sprint 5 more yards.
  4. Walk back. Cut to the other side on the next rep.

How much to do

6 each way to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Jog in. Plant, cut and go.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.
