5-10-5 shuttle

A footwork drill, best from age 9. For a younger athlete it is the last drill of the session, and optional at the weekend.

How to do it

  1. Set three cones in a line, 5 yards apart.
  2. Start at the middle cone in a low stance.
  3. Sprint 5 yards to one side and touch the line with your hand.
  4. Sprint 10 yards to the far cone and touch, then sprint back through the middle.

Why it helps

The 5-10-5 shuttle practices two full changes of direction at speed, with a touch of the line each time. It looks like a defender reacting to a play that shifts side to side.

The usual mistake

Kids often run past the line and turn in a wide circle. Watch for the hips dropping low and a hand touching right at the line before they push back.

Make it easier

Run it at an easy jog first, focusing only on touching the line and turning.

How much to do

6 reps to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Three going right first, three going left first.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week
