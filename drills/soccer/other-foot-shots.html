Other-foot shots

A soccer drill from the Moves and finishing set, best from age 9. For a younger athlete it is the last drill of the session, and optional at the weekend.

How to do it

  1. Set the ball 8 yards from the goal or target.
  2. Use only your weaker foot.
  3. Plant, point your toe down, and strike the middle of the ball.
  4. Low and on target beats hard and wide.

Why it helps

Weaker-foot shots give a player a second way to score, which helps when the ball arrives on the other foot in front of goal.

The usual mistake

Kids try to blast it and the shot goes wide. Remind them that low and on target beats hard and wide.

Make it easier

Move the ball a couple of yards closer and let them aim at the middle of the target.

How much to do

12 shots to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Ball standing still.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 8 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Tuesday is skill day. Soccer has 3 sets of skill drills and uses one a week: Ball control, Passing and Moves and finishing.

Moves and finishing. One good move to get past a defender, then a shot on target.

What you need

A ball that is the right size for your age and a few cones or shoes. A wall helps.

No heading. Young players leave heading out of practice. An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
