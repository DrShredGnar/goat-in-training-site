Shooting

A soccer drill from the Ball control set for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Use a goal, two cones as a gate, or a marked spot on a wall.
  2. Plant your standing foot beside the ball.
  3. Point your toe down and strike the middle of the ball with your laces.
  4. Land on your kicking foot.

Why it helps

Laces shooting trains a clean strike from a stationary ball, like on a free kick or a ball that sits up for them near the goal.

The usual mistake

Kids lean back and the shot sails high. Watch for the toe pointed down, the knee over the ball, and the landing on the kicking foot.

Make it easier

Move a few yards closer and use a bigger target so they can focus on clean contact.

How much to do

15 shots to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: From about 8 yards, ball standing still.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 8 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Tuesday is skill day. Soccer has 3 sets of skill drills and uses one a week: Ball control, Passing and Moves and finishing.

Ball control. Hundreds of touches with both feet. The ball should feel like part of your foot.

What you need

A ball that is the right size for your age and a few cones or shoes. A wall helps.

No heading. Young players leave heading out of practice. An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
