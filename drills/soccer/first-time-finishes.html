First-time finishes

A soccer drill from the Moves and finishing set for ages 11 to 14. In the app it follows the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday.

How to do it

  1. Stand 10 yards from the goal, level with one post. Coach stands to the side with the balls.
  2. Coach rolls a ball across in front of you.
  3. Without stopping it, plant your standing foot beside the ball and strike it low toward the far corner.
  4. Follow your shot in, in case it comes back.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

Why it helps

First-time finishes train a player to strike a moving ball without stopping it, like when a teammate sends a pass across the front of goal.

The usual mistake

Kids plant the standing foot too far behind the ball and the shot rises. Watch for the plant foot right beside the ball.

Make it easier

Have Coach roll the ball even slower and let them aim for the whole goal, not just the far corner.

How much to do

10 shots to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Slow rolls onto your stronger foot.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 8 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Tuesday is skill day. Soccer has 3 sets of skill drills and uses one a week: Ball control, Passing and Moves and finishing.

Moves and finishing. One good move to get past a defender, then a shot on target.

What you need

A ball that is the right size for your age and a few cones or shoes. A wall helps.

No heading. Young players leave heading out of practice. An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
