Defend the line

A soccer drill for Saturday practice for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Coach dribbles toward a line behind you.
  2. Stay sideways-on and low, a step away from the ball.
  3. Back up slowly and steer Coach to one side. Do not dive in.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

How much to do

10 reps to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Coach walks with the ball.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Saturday is full practice, the longest session of the week.

Saturday practice. Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then real soccer.

What you need

A ball that is the right size for your age and a few cones or shoes. A wall helps.

No heading. Young players leave heading out of practice. An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
