How the week is laid out

  • Monday: speed. Train like a sprinter: practise how you run, then short, all-out sprints with full rest. Speed gets built when you are fresh.
  • Tuesday: skill day. The sport's own drills, one set a week.
  • Wednesday: power and body control. Jump, land, crawl and hold. Landing quietly matters as much as jumping far.
  • Thursday: feet and coordination. Quick feet, balance and changing direction. Stay low and stay light.
  • Friday: rest and watch. No running or jumping today. Rest is when your body gets stronger, and smart players win.
  • Saturday: full practice. Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then the sport itself.
  • Sunday: play day. Great athletes grow up playing everything. A different sport is the best choice, or some extra time with their own.
