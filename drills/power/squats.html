Squats

A power drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Feet a little wider than your hips, toes turned slightly out.
  2. Sit your hips back and down like sitting in a chair. Heels stay down.
  3. Go until your thighs are level with the ground, chest up.
  4. To find that depth, put a medicine ball or a low box behind you and touch it lightly each rep. Do not sit on it.
  5. Stand all the way up.

How much to do

3 × 10 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Heels down, chest up.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Squats and becomes Slow squats, Split squats and One-leg bench squats.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week
