Jumps

A power drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Broad jump: swing your arms, jump forward as far as you can, land on both feet and stick it.
  2. Vertical jump: stand next to a wall, dip, and jump straight up to touch as high as you can.
  3. Land softly with bent knees on every jump.

How much to do

3 × 3 broad, 3 × 3 vertical to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Full effort, soft landings.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.
