Broad jumps

A power drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Stand with your toes behind a line, feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Swing your arms back and bend your knees, then throw your arms forward and jump as far as you can.
  3. Land on both feet with your knees bent.
  4. Stick it. No extra steps and no hands on the ground.

How much to do

3 × 3 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: One jump at a time. Stick every landing.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Broad jumps and becomes Double broad jumps and Triple broad jumps.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week
