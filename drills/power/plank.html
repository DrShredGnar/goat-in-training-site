Plank

A power drill for ages 11 to 14. In the app it follows the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday, and from age 12 it is part of every power and body control day.

How to do it

  1. Lie face down, then lift onto your forearms and toes, elbows under your shoulders.
  2. Make one straight line from your head to your heels. Hips not sagging and not sticking up.
  3. Squeeze your stomach and keep breathing. Stop the round when the straight line breaks.

How much to do

3 × 20 s to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Straight as a board. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Plank and becomes Side plank and Plank shoulder taps.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.
