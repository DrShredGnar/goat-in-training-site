Cone hops

A power drill for ages 11 to 14. In the app it follows the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday, and from age 12 it is part of every power and body control day.

How to do it

  1. Set five low cones or rolled-up socks in a row, about two feet apart.
  2. Jump over each one off two feet and land on two feet.
  3. Land softly on the balls of your feet, knees bent and over your toes.
  4. Walk back to the start. That walk is your rest.

How much to do

3 × 5 hops to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Hold each landing for one second before the next jump.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Cone hops and becomes One-leg line hops.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.
