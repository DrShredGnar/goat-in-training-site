Wall hinge

A power drill for ages 12 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Stand with your back to a wall, one foot-length away from it, feet under your hips.
  2. Keep a small bend in your knees. Push your hips straight back until they touch the wall.
  3. Your back stays flat and your knees do not move forward.
  4. Squeeze your backside to stand up tall.

How much to do

3 × 10 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Hips back to the wall with a flat back. Feel it in the back of your legs.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Wall hinge and becomes Hip bridges, One-leg hip bridges and One-leg hinge.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Wednesday is power and body control day.

From age 12. At 12 the power and body control day becomes a 45-minute session that starts with a control warm-up and adds these.

What you need

Nothing extra. An athlete of 12 or older with a loop band and a light medicine ball gets a loaded version in the app.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.

More from age 12

  • Squat jumps From 12

    Hold the half squat for a second, then jump as high as you can. Quiet landing.

  • Floor pulls From 12

    Reach long, pull your elbows to your ribs, squeeze, and reach again.

  • Carries From 12

    One in each hand. Stand tall, shoulders back, no leaning.

  • Chest throws From 12

    Ball at your chest. Step and push it away as fast as you can.
