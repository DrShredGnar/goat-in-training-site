Skater jumps

A power drill, best from age 9. For a younger athlete it is the last drill of the session, and optional on the weekend.

How to do it

  1. Stand on your right leg.
  2. Leap sideways to your left and land on your left leg.
  3. Bend the landing knee and hold your balance.
  4. Leap back the other way.

Why it helps

Skater jumps practice pushing off one leg sideways and landing on the other. It mirrors a defender sliding to cut off a dribbler or a skater pushing across the ice.

The usual mistake

Kids often land and go straight into the next leap without holding. Count "one" out loud on each landing before they push off again.

Make it easier

Make the leaps short, almost a big step, so balance comes first and distance later.

How much to do

2 × 8 each side to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Hold each landing for a full second.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Skater jumps and becomes Skater and hop and Skaters into a sprint.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week
