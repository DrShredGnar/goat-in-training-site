Push-ups

A power drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

It starts as Incline push-ups.

  1. Hands a little wider than your shoulders.
  2. Make your body one straight line from head to heels. Squeeze your stomach and your backside.
  3. Lower your chest until it nearly touches, elbows angled back and not flared out wide.
  4. Push back up without letting your hips sag.

How much to do

2 × 8 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Hands on a bench or step.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Incline push-ups and becomes Slow push-ups.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Wednesday is power and body control day.

Power and body control day. Jump, land, crawl and hold. Landing quietly matters as much as jumping far.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
