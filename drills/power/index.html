Jumping and strength drills for kids

18 jumping, landing and body-weight strength drills for ages 6 to 14, with no gym. Landing quietly is taught before jumping far, and push-ups, squats and hangs are done well before they are done often.

Power and body control day

Jump, land, crawl and hold. Landing quietly matters as much as jumping far.

  • Stick landings

    Step off a low step, land quietly, and freeze for two seconds.

  • Broad jumps

    One jump at a time. Stick every landing.

  • Seated jumps

    Sit still, then jump as high as you can. Land quietly.

  • Pogo hops Best from 9

    Small, quick bounces. Stiff ankles, quiet feet.

  • Skater jumps Best from 9

    Hold each landing for a full second.

  • Animal walks

    Bear crawl, crab walk and frog jumps. 10 yards each.

  • Squats

    Heels down, chest up.

  • Push-ups

    Hands on a bench or step.

  • Bar hang

    Tight grip, still body.

Harder power drills

For athletes of 11 and up. They follow the session on a 45 or 60 minute weekday, and from age 12 they are part of every power and body control day.

  • Cone hops From 11

    Hold each landing for one second before the next jump.

  • Plank From 11

    Straight as a board. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

From age 12

At 12 the power and body control day becomes a 45-minute session that starts with a control warm-up and adds these.

  • Squat jumps From 12

    Hold the half squat for a second, then jump as high as you can. Quiet landing.

  • Wall hinge From 12

    Hips back to the wall with a flat back. Feel it in the back of your legs.

  • Floor pulls From 12

    Reach long, pull your elbows to your ribs, squeeze, and reach again.

  • Carries From 12

    One in each hand. Stand tall, shoulders back, no leaning.

  • Chest throws From 12

    Ball at your chest. Step and push it away as fast as you can.

Saturday practice

Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then the sport itself.

  • Jumps

    Full effort, soft landings.

  • Body-control circuit Best from 9

    Squats 10, incline push-ups 8, bear crawl 10 yards, bar hang 20 seconds, one-leg balance 20 seconds each side.
