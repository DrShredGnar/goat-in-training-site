Seated jumps

A power drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Sit on a bench, a step or a sturdy chair with your feet flat and your knees bent like the corner of a square.
  2. Sit still. No rocking back first.
  3. Throw your arms up and jump as high as you can, straight from the seat.
  4. Land quietly on both feet with your knees bent and over your toes. Sit back down before the next one.

How much to do

3 × 4 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Sit still, then jump as high as you can. Land quietly.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. It starts as Seated jumps and becomes Seated box jumps and Kneeling jumps.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week
