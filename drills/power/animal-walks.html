Animal walks

A power drill for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Bear crawl: on hands and feet, knees an inch off the ground, back flat. Move the opposite hand and foot together.
  2. Crab walk: sit, hands behind you, lift your hips and walk feet first.
  3. Frog jumps: squat low with hands on the ground, jump forward, and land back in the squat.

How much to do

3 rounds to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Bear crawl, crab walk and frog jumps. 10 yards each.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every landing quiet and balanced with knees over toes, and every strength rep clean, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Wednesday is power and body control day.

Power and body control day. Jump, land, crawl and hold. Landing quietly matters as much as jumping far.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
