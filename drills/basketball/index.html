Basketball drills for kids

24 basketball drills a child can do at home with a parent, for ages 6 to 14. Each one has plain steps, a starting amount and one thing to say.

What you need

A basketball that fits your hands. A hoop helps. With no hoop, shoot to a spot high on a wall.

Handles and shot

Dribble with both hands and shoot with the same form every time. Eyes up, not on the ball.

  • Ball wraps

    Around the waist, the knees and the head. 45 seconds each.

  • Pound dribbles

    Waist high. Push the ball down hard and look at Coach, not the ball.

  • Dribble on the move

    Walking. Keep the ball at your side, not in front of your feet.

  • Form shots

    One step from the hoop. A lower hoop is fine. Same form every shot.

  • Layups

    From one step, no dribble. Use the backboard.

  • Passing Best from 9

    From about 6 feet. Step into every pass.

  • Move and pull up From 11

    Crossover at the cone, one dribble, and a shot from close. Go both ways.

Finishing and footwork

Stop on balance, pivot without traveling, and finish close to the hoop with either hand.

  • Jump stops

    Walking speed.

  • Pivots

    Quarter turns, forward and backward.

  • Power layups

    No dribble.

  • Free throws

    From a close line. Same routine every shot.

  • Other-hand layups Best from 9

    From one step, no dribble.

  • Fake and step through Best from 9

    No defender. Fake, step, finish.

  • Finish through a bump From 11

    A light bump, on your strong-hand side.

Pass, defend, rebound

Good teams pass, guard and rebound. These are the jobs that keep you on the floor.

  • Wall passes

    Chest passes.

  • Catch ready

    Coach passes from 6 feet.

  • Closeouts

    Sprint, chop, hand up.

  • Rebound and chin

    Two hands every time.

  • Rebound and outlet Best from 9

    Slow and correct.

  • Give and go Best from 9

    Walk through it first, then jog.

  • Guard the ball From 11

    Coach goes at half speed and changes direction once.

Saturday practice

Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then real basketball.

  • Defensive slides

    Slide across and back.

  • Attack the hoop

    No defender. One or two dribbles, then the layup.

  • Spot shooting Best from 9

    Five spots, close in.

The rest of a basketball week

Three of the four weekday sessions are the same for every sport: speed on Monday, power and body control on Wednesday and footwork on Thursday. Friday is the rest day: a stretch, then watch one quarter and draw one play. Saturday's practice ends with a game, and Sunday is play day: a different sport, or shoot around.
