Spot shooting

A basketball drill for Saturday practice, best from age 9. For a younger athlete it is the last drill of the session, and optional at the weekend.

How to do it

  1. Pick five spots at a distance where your form stays the same.
  2. Coach passes, you catch with your feet set and shoot.
  3. Follow your shot and get the rebound.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

How much to do

3 makes at each spot to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Five spots, close in.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 8 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Saturday is full practice, the longest session of the week.

Saturday practice. Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then real basketball.

What you need

A basketball that fits your hands. A hoop helps. With no hoop, shoot to a spot high on a wall.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
