Form shots

A basketball drill from the Handles and shot set for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Stand close to the hoop, feet pointing at it, knees bent.
  2. Ball on your shooting hand, elbow under the ball, other hand only on the side.
  3. Push up with your legs and snap your wrist. Hold your hand up like reaching into a cookie jar.
  4. The ball should spin backward and drop in softly.

How much to do

15 makes to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: One step from the hoop. A lower hoop is fine. Same form every shot.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 8 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Tuesday is skill day. Basketball has 3 sets of skill drills and uses one a week: Handles and shot; Finishing and footwork; Pass, defend, rebound.

Handles and shot. Dribble with both hands and shoot with the same form every time. Eyes up, not on the ball.

What you need

A basketball that fits your hands. A hoop helps. With no hoop, shoot to a spot high on a wall.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.

More from Handles and shot

  • Ball wraps

    Around the waist, the knees and the head. 45 seconds each.

  • Pound dribbles

    Waist high. Push the ball down hard and look at Coach, not the ball.

  • Dribble on the move

    Walking. Keep the ball at your side, not in front of your feet.

  • Layups

    From one step, no dribble. Use the backboard.

  • Passing Best from 9

    From about 6 feet. Step into every pass.

  • Move and pull up From 11

    Crossover at the cone, one dribble, and a shot from close. Go both ways.
