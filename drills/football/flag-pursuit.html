Flag pursuit

A football drill for Saturday practice for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Coach tucks a flag or a sock into a waistband and moves along a sideline.
  2. Start 10 yards inside, level with Coach.
  3. Run to where Coach is going, not to where Coach is now.
  4. Pull the flag as you run by.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

How much to do

10 reps to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Coach jogs.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: every pattern at a steady rhythm without a miss, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Saturday is full practice, the longest session of the week.

Saturday practice. Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then real football.

What you need

A football and two tennis balls. A flag or a small towel to pull, and a hoop, box or marked spot to throw at.

An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
