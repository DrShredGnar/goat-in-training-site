Ground balls

A baseball and softball drill from the Fielding and swings set for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Feet wide, knees bent, glove open on the ground out in front of you.
  2. Watch the ball all the way into the glove.
  3. Cover it with your throwing hand, like an alligator closing its mouth.
  4. Bring the ball to your belly, then stand up.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

How much to do

15 to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Coach rolls them straight at you.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 9 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Tuesday is skill day. Baseball and softball has 3 sets of skill drills and uses one a week: Fielding and swings, Throwing and Hitting and running.

Fielding and swings. Catch it, throw it, hit it. Clean and easy first, harder later.

What you need

A glove, a soft or tennis ball, a bat, and a few plastic practice balls.

Easy throws only. Stop for the day if an elbow or shoulder hurts. Before any swing, check that nobody is within two bat lengths. An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
