Baseball and softball drills for kids

24 baseball and softball drills a child can do at home with a parent, for ages 6 to 14. Each one has plain steps, a starting amount and one thing to say.

What you need

A glove, a soft or tennis ball, a bat, and a few plastic practice balls.

Easy throws only. Stop for the day if an elbow or shoulder hurts. Before any swing, check that nobody is within two bat lengths.

Fielding and swings

Catch it, throw it, hit it. Clean and easy first, harder later.

  • Play catch

    From about 20 feet. Easy throws. Point, step, throw.

  • Ground balls

    Coach rolls them straight at you.

  • Fly balls

    Easy underhand tosses straight up to you.

  • Wall ball Best from 9

    Throw and field the bounce. Two hands.

  • Swings

    No ball. Slow and balanced: stance, step, swing, finish.

  • Base running Best from 9

    Home to first. Run all the way through the bag.

  • Range plays From 11

    Rolled by hand, three steps away. Six to each side.

Throwing

Good throws come from the feet up. Keep the count low and stop if the arm feels tired or sore.

  • Wrist flicks

    Soft flicks to Coach’s glove.

  • One-knee throws

    From 10 yards.

  • Shuffle and throw

    From 15 yards.

  • Target throws

    From 8 yards.

  • Balance and pitch Best from 9

    From 30 feet. Balance first, then throw.

  • Long toss Best from 9

    Back up slowly.

  • Relay turns From 11

    Coach throws from 20 feet. Turn and throw to a target 20 feet behind you.

Hitting and running

A level swing, a soft bunt and smart feet on the bases.

  • Tee work

    Line drives, not pop-ups.

  • Soft toss

    Slow tosses.

  • Load and stride

    Stop for a second after the step, then swing.

  • Bunting

    Coach tosses underhand from close in, with a soft ball.

  • Rounding first Best from 9

    Jog it to learn the path.

  • Hit it the other way Best from 9

    Off the tee.

  • Swing or take From 11

    Coach makes the balls easy to see: well high or well outside.

Saturday practice

Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then real baseball / softball.

  • Field and throw

    Rolled grounders. Throw back to Coach.

  • Live swings

    Slow pitches. Just make contact.

  • First-step steals Best from 9

    Go on a clap.

The rest of a baseball and softball week

Three of the four weekday sessions are the same for every sport: speed on Monday, power and body control on Wednesday and footwork on Thursday. Friday is the rest day: a stretch, then watch one inning and where's the play?. Saturday's practice ends with a game, and Sunday is play day: a different sport, or backyard catch.
