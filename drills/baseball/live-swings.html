Live swings

A baseball and softball drill for Saturday practice for ages 6 to 14.

How to do it

  1. Use plastic or foam balls.
  2. Coach pitches underhand or overhand from a short distance.
  3. Swing only at pitches between your knees and your chest.

"Coach" is whoever is helping: a parent, an older brother or sister, or a friend.

Why it helps

Live swings put the swing against a real pitch, so your athlete practices seeing the ball from the pitcher's hand like they do in every game at-bat.

The usual mistake

Kids often chase pitches over their head or in the dirt. Remind them to swing only between the knees and chest and to let the rest go.

How much to do

25 pitches to start, at the Rookie level.

What to say: Slow pitches. Just make contact.

How it gets harder

The app has four levels of this drill: Rookie, Starter, Captain and All-Star. The move stays the same and the amount, the distance or the speed goes up.

When to move up: at least 9 clean reps out of 10 in every drill, for two straight weeks. A parent decides.

Where it fits in the week

Saturday is full practice, the longest session of the week.

Saturday practice. Everything in one practice: speed, jumps, body control, then real baseball / softball.

What you need

A glove, a soft or tennis ball, a bat, and a few plastic practice balls.

Easy throws only. Stop for the day if an elbow or shoulder hurts. Before any swing, check that nobody is within two bat lengths. An adult should be there for every session, and stop if anything hurts. This is general training guidance, not medical advice: the Terms of Use have the full safety notes.
