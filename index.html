GOAT in Training runs on your phone. Setting up and signing in both happen inside the app, so there is nothing to log in to on this website.

How to start

  1. Download the app on a parent's phone. The first 10 sessions are free for every athlete.
  2. Add your athlete. A first name or nickname, birth year, sport and goal. You do not need an account to begin.
  3. Train today. Your athlete does the session and checks off each drill.
  4. Want it on a second phone? Sign in from the Parent tab with a code sent to your email, and your athletes and their progress appear on both.

What is inside

  • A short session every day. Speed, power, footwork and your sport's skills, about 15 to 25 minutes on weekdays.
  • Five sports. Football, basketball, baseball and softball, hockey, and soccer.
  • Your athlete checks it off. Every finished session moves the goat up the mountain.
  • Progress you can measure. Levels for each skill and a Combine Day every six weeks.
  • Built for families. No ads, no chat, no public profiles. A parent holds the account.
