Your team

See which of your players are putting in the work at home, and pick the skill drills your team does each week. Free for coaches.

  • Families join with your code. You make a team here and get a six-character code. A parent types it into the app, reads exactly what you will see, and decides.
  • What you see. Each player's name as the parent typed it, sessions finished each week, and how much each Combine Day event improved, as a percent. Never times or distances, never anything about how a child felt, and never a parent's email.
  • What you can do. Pick the week's skill drills for the whole team, or leave the app's usual turn. There is no chat and no way to message a child.

Signing in keeps you signed in on this browser until you sign out. A coach's account holds the email address, the teams' names, your name as families see it, each team's sport and code, and the drills you pick. The privacy policy says the rest.
